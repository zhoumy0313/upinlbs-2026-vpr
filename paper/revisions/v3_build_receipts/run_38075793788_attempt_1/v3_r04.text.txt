   Consequently, this is a working empirical draft, not an
independently validated new-method submission. Correlated
video frames, rounded geographic labels, reference loops, one
limited P backbone grid, unavailable external data and partial
related-paper access restrict the conclusion. Actual evidence
and prior manuscripts are preserved for human review. Further
external validation would require a fresh metadata audit and
prospective freeze, rather than retuning on an opened test.
