# V4 prior art and bounded research decision

Read the preserved V3 LITERATURE_MATRIX.md and PRIMARY_SOURCE_RECEIPTS.json, plus original V2 source-reading notes before implementation. Existing SeqSLAM/OpenSeqSLAM2 establish trajectory/speed searches; STA-VPR (Lu et al. 2021, https://arxiv.org/html/2103.13580v2; author code Lu-Feng/STA-VPR) establishes LM-DTW and endpoint relaxation with local strip features. AdaptSeqVPR (Li et al. IROS2023 DOI10.1109/IROS55552.2023.10341533) establishes dynamic step adjustment and learned trust; only publisher abstract/metadata accessible, not a full reproduction. SMR (Hussaini et al. 2025, https://arxiv.org/html/2503.06840v2 and QVPR/vpr-smr-predictor) establishes supervised temporal trust and rescue/harm reasoning. Full primary source access limits are preserved; this round does not claim an official reproduction of those algorithms.

One tested mechanism: score interpolation on fixed physical-time lags followed by existing-style bounded visual endpoint refinement subject to temporal support. Clock ratios and reference times are allowed; camera positions/rotations/real motion are not. Reverse and stationary ratio hypotheses are given to every time-aware method, including all same-pool simple controls. Five query/reference score samples are bilinearly interpolated using only observations already present at current query time. This is a declared adaptation of temporal alignment ideas, not the first temporal matcher or first endpoint refinement. Pure global descriptors, no local geometry, no model training.

The finite grid is frozen before Pioneer prediction. Strong original short/full windows, old fixed L9, global blends, dual-model single-frame/RRF/affine fusion, naive R1/R2 and time-aware full/global/R1/R2 controls all compete. Proposed support constraint has 36 configurations. No optional mechanism/ablation branch is added; matched-pool constraint removal/fusion already supplies the required ablations. Select only development; artificial sampling shifts are not a second collection. If both strict radii do not beat the strongest baseline, keep the negative study and do not invent additional modules. Even a small strict improvement is not proof of algorithmic novelty or generalizable significance.
